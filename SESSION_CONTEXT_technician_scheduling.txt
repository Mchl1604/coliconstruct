1. WHAT WAS BUILT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: take a technician off a project on a future date (or for some days) without
losing any history.

Two places change a team, and they mean different things:

A) Project Details > Assigned Team (the "master control")
   - Changes the team as it stands TODAY. No effective date.
   - Whoever is removed comes off COMPLETELY: their current span closes today and
     any later span (a return from days off, a scheduled start) is deleted.
   - Save flow: Save -> lead-replacement confirm (if the lead changes) -> preview
     (POST /projects/{id}/team/preview) -> review step listing stranded tasks with
     Reassign / Unassign choices ("Keep" is NOT offered, because it takes effect today) -> save.

B) Technicians page > Schedules tab > pick technician > pick a project > "Remove Schedule"
   Two modes:
   - "These days" (mode=days): off From..To, back the day after.
       * Lead technician -> must pick a STAND-IN lead (heading "Choose a Stand-in Lead")
         who leads exactly those days.
   - "From a date onward" (mode=from): off from that day for good.
       * Lead technician -> must pick a replacement who takes over from that day.
   - Earliest day = today (business today, Asia/Manila). Past days refused.
   - Tasks dated into the removed days need a decision: Reassign / Leave unassigned /
     Keep and flag it (keep is allowed only for future-dated changes).
   - Cannot remove the last technician on the project.

Decisions the user made (don't re-ask):
   - Master control: immediate, no date. Technicians page: date range, or from a date on.
   - Lead days off: pick a stand-in lead. Earliest day: today onward.
   - Strict task rule: a task's holder must be on the team for the WHOLE task (start..due)
     within ONE continuous span. Dates outside are greyed out / cards blocked.
   - Only one lead on any day, and a lead on every day from the change onward.
   - Upcoming members can view the project (read-only) before their start, but can't run it.
   - Cancelling either half of a lead handover/cover cancels both.
   - Project completion/cancellation cancels scheduled changes.
   - Reports: Role, Assignment Status (Active/Leaving/Upcoming/Removed + Replaced by),
     Start Date, Removal Date columns.
   - Bulk lead replace on Technicians page: unassigns affected tasks after a
     one-sentence warning.

2. DATA MODEL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
tbl_project_technicians = one row per continuous SPAN of membership (never overlapping):
   joined_at   inclusive first day
   removed_at  EXCLUSIVE - the first day NOT on the team (null = open-ended)
   team_role   snapshot of role (lead_technician/technician) - history of who led
   removal_recorded_at  when the removal was decided (for history ordering)
Stored times are Manila wall-clock (Schedule::businessNow()).
Days off = the span closes at From, and a new span starts at To+1 (keeping the old end).
Stand-in lead = a lead span from From until the lead's return day.
Migrations: 2026_09_14_090000_add_team_role..., 2026_09_14_100000_add_removal_recorded_at...

Key scopes/relations: ProjectTechnician::current / notEnded / upcoming / coveringPeriod;
Project::projectTechnicians (current), rosterTechnicians (not ended), upcomingTechnicians,
teamHistory; Project::leadOn(date), isLeadMember, scheduledChangeLabel, scheduledEndLabel,
isLeadCover; Project::crewOn(date).
Task gap: Task::GAP_OFF_TEAM "Technician Not Assigned for Dates" (kept-and-flagged tasks).
